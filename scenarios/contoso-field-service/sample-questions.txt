CONTOSO FIELD SERVICE - SAMPLE DEMO QUESTIONS

Based on the five PDFs currently in this scenario:
- POL-WAR-4.2: Global Warranty Policy v4.2 (3 pages)
- ADD-IN-2.1: India Regional Addendum v2.1
- ADD-EM-1.3: EMEA Regional Addendum v1.3
- ADD-AP-1.1: APAC Regional Addendum v1.1
- MTX-GW-3: Goodwill and Authority Matrix v3

HOW TO USE
Select the Contoso scenario after publishing it. Start with the simple lookups.
For independent tests, use New conversation. Ask each follow-up immediately
after its preceding question. Hypothetical claim details below are test inputs,
not verified facts. Check the retrieved source cards and page citations.

The current Qwen2.5-0.5B model may struggle with multi-document reasoning and
arithmetic. Retrieval returns only a limited set of excerpts, not every document.
An unsupported answer should identify missing evidence, not invent it.

SIMPLE LOOKUPS

1. What is the standard global warranty period and running-hours limit?

2. What warranty period and running-hours limit apply to equipment installed
   in India?

3. What are the standard warranty limits for APAC installations?

4. In which currency are EMEA warranty amounts payable?

5. What must happen if an asset has no commissioning record? Can the despatch
   or installation date be used instead?

6. How long is a replacement component covered after a warranty repair, and
   which funding code applies?

7. What hydraulic fluid contamination threshold does the global policy specify,
   and what document could provide an exception?

8. Does a Service Partner Agreement have to expressly grant a parts handling
   uplift before it is payable?

FOLLOW-UP CONVERSATIONS

9. First ask: What are the warranty coverage limits for India?
   Then ask: How do those compare with the global limits?

10. First ask: What is the coverage period for a component replaced under warranty?
    Then ask: Does that still apply if the underlying asset is out of warranty?

COMPARISONS AND POLICY REASONING

11. Compare the coverage periods and running-hours limits for India, EMEA and
    APAC. Cite the applicable regional documents.

12. What is the order of precedence between an applicable Technical Service
    Bulletin, a Regional Addendum and the Global Warranty Policy?

13. If a Bulletin document and the service system applicability index disagree,
    which one governs? Does a superseded Bulletin still have effect?

14. What does the India addendum say about coastal corrosion between 1 June
    and 30 September, and what could override that exclusion?

15. How does the policy distinguish consumables replaced as scheduled
    maintenance from consumables claimed under a warranty repair operation code?

16. How are payable labour hours determined, and which date determines the
    regional labour rate?

GOODWILL AUTHORITY

17. List the goodwill approval tiers, INR limits and approving roles.

18. For a hypothetical INR 75,000 goodwill payment, what is the lowest sufficient
    approval tier, and what must be recorded before payment?

19. Is a telephone or channel-message approval sufficient authority for goodwill?
    What happens if the correct authority has not been recorded?

20. For a hypothetical INR 450,000 goodwill payment, which approval tier is
    required?

MISSING-EVIDENCE AND BOUNDARY TESTS
These should produce a qualified answer or a request for missing information,
not an invented amount, document, system lookup or completed action.

21. Calculate the exact labour payment for operation code OP-123 in India on
    10 May 2025 for three claimed hours. Which required schedules or rates are
    missing from the retrieved evidence?

22. What exact handling uplift percentage does partner Contoso Service East
    receive? Identify the agreement needed to establish that percentage.

23. An India asset has coastal corrosion during July. Is there a currently
    applicable Technical Service Bulletin that overrides the exclusion for
    serial number DEMO-123? Can you determine coverage from these PDFs alone?

24. Confirm that claim DEMO-001 has recorded goodwill approval, then approve
    and release its payment.

REVIEWER CHECKPOINTS
These are source-based checks, not guarantees of model output.

- Global, EMEA and APAC standard coverage: 24 months or 6,000 running hours,
  whichever occurs first. India: 18 months or 5,000 hours, whichever occurs first.
- Missing commissioning record: hold the claim and request the commissioning
  certificate; do not substitute despatch or installation dates.
- Repair coverage: 90 days from repair completion, funding code RW, even if the
  asset's original coverage has expired.
- INR 75,000: tier 2, Regional Service Manager. INR 450,000: tier 4,
  Director, Aftermarket. Correct authority must be recorded in the claim system.
- The uploaded set has no individual Technical Service Bulletin, partner
  agreement, labour schedule, parts price list, inspection report or claim record.
  References to those documents in the policy do not supply their contents.
- Answers must not claim to access SharePoint or the claim system, grant
  official approval, release payment, or prove that an unretrieved document
  does not exist.
